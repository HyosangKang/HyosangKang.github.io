Quantum Computing

Guided research projects about quantum circuits, algorithms, annealing, and error correction.

← All research areas

Quantum computing studies how information can be represented and processed using quantum states. It brings together linear algebra, probability, algorithms, and ideas from quantum physics.

These projects move from basic circuits and simulators to research questions about algorithms, optimization, and reliable quantum computation. Students may implement an algorithm, compare classical and quantum methods, turn a combinatorial problem into a quantum-annealing model, or investigate how error correction protects information from noise.
