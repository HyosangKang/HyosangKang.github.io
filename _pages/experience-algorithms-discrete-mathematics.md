<p class="project-intro">
  Discrete mathematics studies objects built from separate pieces, such as
  graphs, networks, arrangements, and finite sets. Algorithms give precise
  steps for solving questions about those objects.
</p>

The projects in this area connect mathematical structure with computation.
Students can study how to divide resources fairly, colour a graph, create a
schedule, generate pseudorandom numbers, classify game data, or test the limits
of computation. A typical project asks not only whether a method works, but why
it works, how efficient it is, and where it can fail.
