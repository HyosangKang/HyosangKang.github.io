<p class="project-intro">
  Mathematical modelling turns a real situation into variables, assumptions,
  equations, and computational rules. A model helps us explain a system, test
  possible changes, and make predictions.
</p>

The projects here study systems from biology, public health, buildings, and
energy networks. Students decide which parts of the system matter, build and
run a model, compare its behaviour with evidence, and explain its limits. The
research is therefore as much about choosing and checking assumptions as it is
about solving equations.
