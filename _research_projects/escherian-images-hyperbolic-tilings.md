question: How can one motif repeat across a hyperbolic tiling without gaps or overlaps?
goals:
  - Construct a symmetric tiling in the Poincaré disk.
  - Transform a chosen motif into an Escher-like repeating image.
method_intro: Use reflections and hyperbolic isometries to copy one decorated tile.
method:
  - Choose a regular hyperbolic tiling and draw its fundamental polygon.
  - Design a motif whose boundary matches neighbouring copies.
  - Apply the tiling symmetries, clip the copies, and render the full disk.
