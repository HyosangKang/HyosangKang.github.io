question: When does complex data behave as if it lies on a simpler curved space?
goals:
  - Identify manifold-like structure in a natural or social example.
  - Compare local coordinates with an ordinary flat description.
method_intro: Study a complex system through small neighbourhoods that may look low-dimensional.
method:
  - Choose data or a network whose states have many recorded variables.
  - Build local coordinates or neighbourhood graphs and estimate their dimension.
  - Compare distances, clusters, or paths before and after the manifold description.
