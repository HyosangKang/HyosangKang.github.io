  - Construct one number system or mathematical object from simpler foundations.
  - Prove that the required operations and laws are well defined.

method:
  - Choose the starting axioms and the target object.
  - Define the construction, equality, and arithmetic or structural operations.
  - Verify closure, consistency, and the main laws through proofs and examples.
