question: Which geodesic paths return to where they started on a modular surface?
goals:
  - Connect closed geodesics with matrices in the modular group.
  - Find and draw examples inside a fundamental region.
method_intro: Follow a hyperbolic geodesic while identifying points related by modular transformations.
method:
  - Work in the upper-half-plane model and construct a fundamental domain.
  - Represent side crossings by Möbius transformations or symbolic words.
  - Search for returning paths and verify closure from the associated matrix.
