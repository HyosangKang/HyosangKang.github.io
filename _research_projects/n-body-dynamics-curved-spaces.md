question: How does the curvature of space change the motion of interacting bodies?
goals:
  - Formulate an N-body model on a sphere or hyperbolic surface.
  - Simulate orbits and examine conserved quantities.
method_intro: Replace flat-space distance and motion with those determined by a curved metric.
method:
  - Choose coordinates, a metric, and an interaction potential on the curved space.
  - Derive the constrained equations of motion.
  - Integrate the system numerically and compare it with a flat-space case.
